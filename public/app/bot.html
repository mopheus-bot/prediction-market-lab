Trading mode paper
On Kalshi, a "fill" means the bot bought (or sold) a YES/NO contract at a market price. paper = log only, no real money. real = place actual orders on Kalshi's exchange. off = no new trades; the WS keeps streaming so you can see state. Kill switch is the panic button — flips the bot into a state where every new trade is rejected with reason "kill_switch_active".
panic-stop: refuses all new trades immediately. No auto-close of open positions.
Caps & today
Hard limits on Kalshi exposure. per-trade cap = max cents the bot will deploy on a single fill. Daily cap = max cents in any 24h rolling window. Per-vertical overrides let you tune each vertical independently.
